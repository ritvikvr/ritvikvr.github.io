Research

Ongoing

Graph learning for multi-UAV coordination

Unmanned aerial vehicles
Problem

This research develops a dynamic, graph-based learning framework for coordinating fleets of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). Graph neural networks learn how to assign tasks efficiently and how to coordinate without a central controller, while handling changing task assignments, collision avoidance and safe navigation in complex environments.

Significance

The aim is to make multi-UAV missions more efficient, scalable, robust and safe, while cutting the computing load on any central system. The framework is to be tested in simulation and in real-world multi-UAV experiments across a range of operating conditions.

  • Graph neural networks
  • Multi-UAV coordination
  • Dynamic task allocation
  • Decentralized control
  • Collision avoidance
  • Safe navigation
